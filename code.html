About Me

As a PhD in Engineering from UTN Buenos Aires, my research focuses on developing efficient algorithms for phenotype-genotype association, with applications in 3D morphometry, medicine, and forensic science. After obtaining my degree in Informatics from UNPSJB, I joined the Human Biology Research Group (GIBEH) at IPCSH-CENPAT-CONICET as a researcher. Currently, I also collaborate with the University of Jaén (Spain) on innovative algorithms for computational archaeology, driven by a passion for applying engineering to complex biological and historical data.
